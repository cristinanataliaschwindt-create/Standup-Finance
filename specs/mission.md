# Mission

Our mission is to empower users in high-inflation economies to protect and grow their purchasing power. 

We achieve this by lowering the barriers to entry for investment and decentralized finance (DeFi). Standup Finance automatically transforms the savings derived from smart, everyday product substitutions into on-chain micro-investments. By seamlessly integrating into daily consumption habits, we turn what would otherwise be a loss of capital into an automated, yield-generating asset—without requiring prior crypto knowledge or extra disposable income.
